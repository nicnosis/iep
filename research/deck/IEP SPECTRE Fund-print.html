SECTION I
INVESTMENT THESIS
The Thesis

Natural gas is the bridge fuel of the American energy decade —
and its price is not yet telling the truth.

US reserves are the largest in the world, domestic prices sit near historic lows relative to crude, and the spread to European delivery is structurally wide. We buy that mispricing three ways.

#1

~3–5×

SECTION III
PREMIERE TRANSACTION
The Premiere Transaction

A quiet position in the #1 refueler of CNG & LNG for long-haul trucking.

The target's equity sits near multi-year lows alongside depressed domestic gas pricing — a structural mispricing we believe closes as industrial demand, exports, and on-site power generation expand. Our approach is staged and disciplined.

“If we cannot understand it or explain it in simple terms, we will not buy it or invest in it.” — IEP Investment Charter
PHASE 01
Stealth accumulation, <5% position
Open Market
Below 13D disclosure threshold; avoid signaling to the market while the basis is constructive.
PHASE 02
Engage management; build coalition
Private
Constructive dialogue on capital allocation, cost of capital, and the asset's strategic option value.
PHASE 03
Hostile bid if value gap persists
Board / Shareholders
Targeting a re-rating of ≥3× on the realised basis, in line with peer-group historical multiples.
PHASE 04
Integrate refueling into IEP power & data strategy
Operational
Convert refueling footprint into a platform for on-site data-center power and EV corridors.

Municipal Data Centers

What do we do with surplus US gas?
Power the grid we actually need.

IEP is in discussions with municipalities to develop proprietary, fuel-agnostic data centers — sized for local demand, owned under local governance, and paid for through long-duration offtake.
